# Cadentrail

**A DAW for YuE2**

Created by **Madiator2011**. Built for **Runpod**.

Cadentrail is an independent browser workstation and Runpod template. It is not an official YuE2 or Runpod product, and no endorsement is implied.

The music engine is **m-a-p/YuE2-3B**, by the YuE2 / Multimodal Art Projection team, with the official YuE2 inference package and YuE2-Vae. Credit for the music model belongs to its authors. See [YuE2](https://huggingface.co/m-a-p/YuE2-3B) and the [third-party notices](docs/THIRD-PARTY.md).

YuE2 model weights are offered under **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Artwork, lyric assistance, alignment, separation and other dependencies retain their own licenses. App branding does not change those terms or grant commercial model rights.

Earlier releases used the working title “YuE2 Studio.” Existing project schemas, persistent storage paths and browser preferences are retained for compatibility.

Mothersuperior: instrumental AR and Hum-to-Song adapters (CC BY-NC 4.0); librosa contributors: local pYIN melody analysis (ISC). Exact sources, modifications and notices are in [THIRD-PARTY.md](docs/THIRD-PARTY.md).
Runpod maintains the [official Runpod agent skills](https://github.com/runpod/runpod-plugins-official) (Apache-2.0). Cadentrail links to that project and includes its own cadentrail-runpod companion skill by Madiator2011; it does not vendor the official plugin.
