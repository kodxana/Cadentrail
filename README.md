Create songs, shape them in Studio, and enjoy your music — all in your browser.

## [Launch on Runpod →](https://console.runpod.io/hub/template/dlh907x9dx?ref=vfker49t)

No local GPU or installation needed. Core music models are included; optional models ask before downloading.

## What’s inside

- **Create** — Describe your sound, write or generate lyrics, and compare takes.
- **Studio** — Arrange tracks, edit scores, mix, separate stems, master and export.
- **Listen** — Enjoy your Library with artwork and synchronized lyrics.
- **Radio** — Describe a live station, hear original songs with crossfades, and change its direction while it plays.
- **Visuals** — Create cover artwork and music videos.
- **Agents** — Bring your own Codex, Claude or another compatible MCP assistant.

Create and Studio share the same project and history. Radio’s temporary songs stay out of your Library. Desktop and mobile layouts are included.

YuE2 generates complete takes. Studio arranges and mixes those recordings, imported audio, MIDI preview instruments and separated stems. MIDI/ABC can guide a new take; per-track AI instrument rendering is unavailable, and exact MIDI reproduction is not guaranteed. See [what reaches the model](docs/GENERATION-EXPECTATIONS.md#studio-and-the-model).

## Get started

1. Open the [Runpod template](https://console.runpod.io/hub/template/dlh907x9dx?ref=vfker49t). Choose **Secure Cloud** and an **RTX 4090 with 24 GB VRAM** (24 GB+ VRAM recommended).
2. Deploy, then open the **HTTP service on port 8000**.
3. Follow the creation flow. Open **Guide** for help and optional tours.
